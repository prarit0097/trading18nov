## Strategy interpretation

The tested rule is the repository's default **long 15-minute opening-range breakout using closed 5-minute candles**. At the snapshot, the last closed candle remained below $162.55 and `trigger` was null. A fetched quote or forming candle alone does not establish the closed-candle trigger. The scanner independently returned no candidate. The correct observed path was to wait, so no hypothetical trade, stop, target, quantity or profit forecast was manufactured.

The $159.58 quote, $159.55 forming candle, $159.64 news move snapshot and $159.77 closed candle refer to different samples or candle states; they are not interchangeable execution prices. Quote parsing uses Yahoo's `regularMarketTime`; this test did not independently measure feed latency or compare synchronized exchange ticks. Yahoo access is not proof of exchange-grade real-time accuracy. Results apply only to the recorded snapshot and can change on later bars.
