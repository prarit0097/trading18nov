# Trade 1 · Delta demo campaign

Start on the owner's explicit **start** instruction. Use the existing Delta India testnet account and the connected `crypto-trader-skills` workflow. Start the campaign at **0 of 10,000 verified trades and 0 of 1,000 reviews**. Each verified trade must open and close exactly three contracts. The aim is to improve both the probability of a profitable close and the average net USD profit per trade, after commissions and funding, within the agreed risk limits. Report the measured outcome and its uncertainty even when the starting strategy performs best.

Use campaign ID `trade1`, its dedicated order journal and review history under `runs/trade1`, and the workbook `outputs/01a1df70-6789-76b0-9431-f0c905a7987a/trade1.xlsx`. Each resume continues from the persisted state. Keep the original `trade.xlsx` as historical evidence. Only positions opened by this campaign's journal can earn a new trade number. Earlier trades and manual, external or hypothetical activity earn zero credit toward either target. Include earlier same-day losses and any account exposure in preflight risk. The historic XRP exit is settled.

The campaign manifest records baseline policy `delta-demo-five-coin-v6+trade1-3c-10m-v2`. Its frozen source and cost assumptions are in `runs/trade1/baseline-3c.zip`. At activation check the environment, position and open-order inventory, current fills and wallet, journal integrity, ledger export, campaign and review counts, remaining quotas and worker ownership. Resolve any ambiguous prior submission before another order for that exposure. A restart must preserve the original first fill time, deadline, outstanding close intent and verified count.

## Entries, exposure and exits

- Select from BTCUSD, SOLUSD, ETHUSD, DOGEUSD and XRPUSD perpetuals on `india_testnet`. Verify the underlying and live product specifications. Rank the feasible combinations of four distinct coins by market turnover. Use recent USD turnover, fresh bid/ask, spread and bounded IOC entry prices as liquidity checks. Recheck fees, available margin and remaining group risk before sending. Log the five-minute closed-bar entry observation, including WAIT when the batch policy admits it, and the exact entry policy version.
- Plan four concurrent positions of **three contracts per coin**. Record each actual fill independently. Exchanges can fill only part of a batch. Reconcile partials and residual exposure before another batch and count a lot only when all three contracts have actually opened and closed. At the campaign finish, cap the final batch at the number still needed.
- Maximum **planned** loss is $10 per position including fees. Maximum daily loss plus reserved open risk is $30 per IST day. Leverage is at most 5× and the combined margin budget is 80% of available margin. Report actual slippage and any realized overrun. Check stop-loss and target coverage on the exchange.
- Each position gets its own ten-minute clock from its **first confirmed exchange entry fill**. Save the UTC timestamp and deadline. A protective fill can exit earlier. At the deadline submit a reduce-only market exit for the remaining owned quantity. Record submission delay and the exchange's actual close time. On recovery check live size, fills, brackets and outstanding exit intents before acting. Reconcile late fills and recover overdue exits without duplicating exposure.
- Reconcile entry and exit fills, order IDs, fees, funding and wallet cashflows. Mark missing evidence as pending. Count each fully settled three-contract position once. Keep partial or unresolved activity visible, including its risk. A workbook refresh reuses durable evidence and cannot create a trade.

## Team and learning

The CEO owns the limits, decision log and deployable skill version. The profitability manager owns the research queue, ten-trade review and recommendation. Assign five specialists to (1) market data, liquidity and universe selection, (2) entries, protective exits and duration, (3) sizing, margin, slippage, fees and funding, (4) execution, fills and restart recovery, and (5) independent reconciliation, validation and statistical uncertainty. Run independent work in parallel and in waves when agent slots are limited. Preserve all five findings, the manager's recommendation and the CEO's decision alongside the review evidence.

1. For each consecutive group of ten verified closes, answer **did this group make money after all costs?** Record gross and net P&L, total costs, wins/losses/breakevens, win rate, mean net P&L, best/worst result, peak-to-trough drawdown and net P&L excluding the largest winner. Check coin, batch, signal, market conditions, strategy version and execution quality. Treat correlated positions in one batch as related observations.
2. For each actual entry, track 15- and 30-minute hypothetical exits using the same position, stop and target. Use the same actual early protective exit where applicable. State execution, fee, slippage and funding assumptions, paired coverage and missing data. Compare win rate and mean net return on matched entries. These estimates stay outside the verified trade tally and realized P&L.
3. For a profitable group, give the supported decision research credit and state what would reproduce or disprove it. For a losing or flat group, distinguish avoidable errors, execution costs and ordinary variation, and identify what was knowable before entry. Record a testable correction where evidence permits. Ten closes trigger a review. Qualification uses independent evidence.
4. Freeze the candidate rule, baseline, test period, cost assumptions, risk limits and promotion criteria before validation. Test on fresh non-overlapping periods and account for batch and day correlation. Report the sample size, effect and uncertainty in after-cost win probability and average net P&L, plus the drawdown impact. The CEO may authorize promotion with reproducible evidence that both outcomes improve within risk limits. Keep the previous version available for rollback. If evidence is inconclusive, retain the baseline and record the finding.
5. Key reviews to specific trade IDs and reconciled evidence. Preserve the original membership and revision trail if late wallet data changes an outcome. Count each reviewed group once. After a restart process outstanding reviews, including the final group at 10,000.

## Workbook, operating limits and completion

Use the workbook to show campaign progress, realized results, open or unresolved positions, risk and every ten-trade review. Record instrument, side, planned and filled size, strategy version, batch and order IDs, entry, leverage, stop, target, fee-inclusive planned risk, first fill, deadline, close submission and fill, reason, delay, fees, funding and net P&L. Display clear time zones and USD units. Label modeled exits as estimates and leave unknown results blank.

Once the owner starts this campaign, keep monitoring when positions are protected, settlements are pending, fewer than four coins qualify, margin is insufficient or the IST daily budget is exhausted. Recheck as conditions change, keeping risk limits intact. At 50% or less verified weekly Codex quota remaining, checkpoint, stop new entries across workers and keep the exit monitor and exchange-side protection active for existing exposure. Resume from the checkpoint when verified weekly remaining exceeds 50%. Record unavailable readings and their effect on entry eligibility. This campaign uses the owner's weekly quota rule. Apply the owner's separate five-hour reserve when doing coding work.

Finish after **10,000 distinct verified demo trades and 1,000 documented CEO-reviewed groups**. Reconcile the workbook against the journal, fills and wallet, resolve residual exposure, and report baseline versus final performance with the data periods, changes promoted or rejected and remaining uncertainty.

## Starting and recovering the run

From the project root, a read-only preflight is `uv run --no-project --with mcp python -m crypto_skills.delta_ledger --campaign trade1 --preview-batch`. On the owner's start, verify the preflight, Codex quota, and sole ownership of the observer. After confirming the owner has resumed, clear the pause markers `runs/trade1/STOP` and `runs/trade1/ENTRY_HOLD`. Verify the three-contract setting before enabling entries. Run the observer with `uv run --no-project --with mcp python -m crypto_skills.delta_observer --campaign trade1 --execute-batch-demo --until-stopped --node "<bundled Node executable>"`. Resolve the executable with the Codex workspace dependency tool. A current margin shortfall leaves the worker waiting for eligibility.

Use the shared observer and reconciliation locks to detect a duplicate process. After a hard crash, check the recorded owner PID and actual running processes before recovering an abandoned lock. Compare exchange inventory and journaled close intents before enabling entries. Track refresh duration as history grows. If reconciliation or workbook export runs beyond its budget, checkpoint and correct the bottleneck while preserving open-position protection. After a recoverable export failure the trade journal remains authoritative. Submit the team's evidence-bound review through `python -m crypto_skills.delta_ledger --campaign trade1 --record-manager-review REPORT.json`.
