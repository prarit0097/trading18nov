# FMZ #5530 original author source check

RafaelZioni's original open-source TradingView strategy was published September 16, 2018. The visible **Source code** tab assigns `b = v < -0.8` and passes that Boolean series as the second operand of the short `crossunder` call. The long side separately passes `z`, the numeric `0.8` constant. The FMZ archive preserves this distinction. The publisher's saved TradingView chart is BITMEX:XBTUSD at one hour. That chart differs from the later FMZ configuration. [RafaelZioni’s original script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/UfLWnWQ6-Inverse-Fisher-RSI-MTF2/)

On October 9, 2018, commenter mark-sch shared a Pine v3 revision that turns the Boolean into a numeric series of ones and zeros, and separately disables security lookahead. RafaelZioni's reply acknowledges repainting. The reply does not adopt the numeric rewrite or establish original coercion behavior. The same author's alert study also discusses repainting without identifying a substitute short threshold. [Original strategy comments](https://www.tradingview.com/script/UfLWnWQ6-Inverse-Fisher-RSI-MTF2/) [Author’s alert study](https://www.tradingview.com/script/XDJWADe9-Inverse-Fisher-RSI-MTF2-alerts/)

The previous local experiments already show that a Boolean-to-number conversion and a numeric −0.8 threshold produce different ETH short signals. The author's source rules out attributing the latter change to RafaelZioni. Exact legacy host coercion and timing remain unresolved.
