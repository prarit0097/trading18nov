## Workbook interpretation

The **Delta backtests** tab shows win rate, gross and net P&L, trading fees, modeled execution costs, and drawdown. Zero-close trials leave the win-rate cells blank. Drawdown uses closed-position cash flows and the equity reference in the respective model. Protocol variants with different data and execution settings keep separate labels. The **Test coverage** tab keeps the exact reason a result remains pending. Delta Demo entry stayed paused during the offline work.

Pending inputs include original bot deployment settings and callbacks, legacy compiler and intrabar dependencies, historical execution state for active limit orders, and instrument-specific protocol history. Each held-open case retains its actual source or data dependency in the audit.
